HOMEWALLET — PRIVACY POLICY Last updated: 2026-08-14 HomeWallet is a personal and shared expense-management application that helps users track expenses, income, transactions, groups, and financial summaries. 1. INFORMATION WE PROCESS Depending on how you use HomeWallet, the application may process: • Mobile number used for account authentication and OTP verification. • Profile information, such as the name provided during account setup. • Group information, including group names, memberships, ownership information, and invite codes. • Expense information and transaction details entered by the user. • Income information entered by the user. • Transaction history and information needed to display dashboards, history, reports, and financial summaries. • Information required to maintain authentication/session state and provide application functionality. 2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION HomeWallet uses information to: • Authenticate users and provide account access. • Create and maintain user profiles. • Create and manage groups and group memberships. • Support invitation and group-joining functionality. • Record, display, edit, and delete expenses and income according to the application's rules. • Display transaction history, dashboards, reports, and financial summaries. • Maintain data consistency between users who share a group. • Protect the security and integrity of the application. 3. AUTHENTICATION HomeWallet uses mobile-number/OTP authentication. A user's mobile number is used to initiate authentication and verify access to the account. 4. GROUPS AND SHARED DATA HomeWallet supports shared groups. Information entered into a shared group may be visible to other authorized members according to the application's access and privacy rules. Invite codes are used to allow users to join groups. Users should treat invite codes as private and share them only with people they intend to invite. 5. FINANCIAL INFORMATION HomeWallet is a tracking and organization tool for personal/shared expenses and income. Based on the current application functionality, it does not provide banking, lending, payment-transfer, investment-trading, insurance, or financial-advisory services. 6. DATA STORAGE AND SERVICE PROVIDERS HomeWallet uses backend/cloud services to authenticate users and store application data so that information can persist across sessions and devices. The final published policy must be kept accurate if backend, analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or other third-party services are added or changed. 7. DATA SHARING Information may be visible to other authorized members when users use shared-group functionality, subject to the application's access and privacy rules. Any third-party service that receives user data should be identified in the final published version of this policy. 8. DATA SECURITY Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. 9. DATA RETENTION Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide HomeWallet functionality, maintain account records, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable terms. 10.
